Each answer updates the championship probabilities and reveals whether a result guarantees a team's qualification or elimination, regardless of how every other game finishes. A clinching result guarantees qualification, but it isn't necessarily the only way a team can qualify. The percentages above the team buttons show ESPN FPI win probabilities, with the favorite on the left and the underdog on the right.
